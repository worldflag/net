WorldFlags started as a reference archive for designers, publishers and educators who needed accurate flag artwork fast — and got tired of low-res, mislabeled downloads.

01 — What we do

A free preview archive, backed by licensed originals

Every flag on this site is shown in a high-resolution, watermarked preview so you can confirm colours, proportions and details before you commit. When you're ready for the real file — up to 13000 × 8014px — we hand off to Shutterstock, where the licensed, watermark-free artwork lives.

We catalogue each flag with its official aspect ratio and a short, source-checked description, the same way a vexillological reference plate would — because "looks about right" isn't good enough for a print run or a broadcast graphic.

02 — Where the artwork comes from

Public domain, sourced carefully

National flags are official state symbols and are, in essentially every country, free of copyright restriction as public-domain artwork. Our preview files are drawn from open repositories such as Wikimedia Commons and Flagpedia, which distribute vector-accurate flags (SVG/PDF/AI) under public-domain or free-use terms — so what you see here is the same shape and colour data used by cartographers and publishers worldwide.
